# Авторская графика

Создано встроенным инструментом ImageGen для этой игры.

- `bogatyri.png` — панорамная иллюстрация меню.
- `portraits.png` — три индивидуальных портрета в одном изображении; интерфейс кадрирует их CSS.

Это растровые иллюстрации. Игровые 3D-модели и варианты доспехов создаются процедурно в `lib/scene.ts`.

## Запрос для панорамы

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ultra-wide cinematic background art for a premium dark fantasy Slavic medieval 3D multiplayer fighting game called Bogatyri. Very high detail photorealistic cinematic illustration. Three realistic medieval Russian bogatyr heroes standing together on the right two thirds, from knees up: center-right Ilya Muromets, broad mature bearded warrior, intricately engraved steel lamellar plate and chainmail, conical silver helmet with gold ridge and nose guard, dark red cape, enormous mace and engraved round shield. Young blond Alyosha Popovich, chainmail and green cloak with a bow. Dobrynya Nikitich, auburn beard, conical helmet, blue-grey cloak and sword. Natural anatomy, authentic metal, dark leather, embroidery. Left third atmospheric empty landscape: distant medieval wooden Russian fortress, riverside, pine forest, olive-grey hills. Pale desaturated warm grey sky, fog and cinematic golden rim light. Restrained charcoal, olive, gold steel and subdued red. Museum-quality painterly realism. No low poly, no cartoon, no text, no logos, no UI, no watermarks.
